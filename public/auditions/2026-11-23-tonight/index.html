🎧 Tonight's biome listening room

Seven experimental listening pages, one place to find them. Each lab plays a synchronized excerpt of the original instrumental stems and lets you compare its processing with the dry music. Nothing here is merged into the shipping game; your ears are the acceptance gate.

Suggested route: try Steampunk first, then Crystal's stronger air shimmer, then Garbage's new musical clangs. After that, revisit the experiments you've already liked. For each, note anything too weak, too loud, or musically distracting.

Continuous sled-speed piston chug; little gear clicks on actual recorded musical accents; real takeoff steam hiss and landing KLA-CHUNK. Gears react, but the artificial beat grid is not used.

Other biome experiments

🐝 Micro Mountain

Music in a tiny plastic enclosure opens when you jump. Actual background bees provide stereo buzz and a physical box rattle follows landing.

Previously liked: boxiness 135% · bee intensity 150%Listen to Micro

🌊 Underwater Mountain

Increasing pressure compresses and muffles the original music as you descend; jumping briefly lifts the pressure, and landing triggers a water-hammer wave.

Try: descend deep · jump/apex · fall · landing pressure waveListen to Underwater
