🎧 Tonight's biome listening room

Twelve experimental listening pages, one place to find them. Each lab plays a synchronized excerpt of the original instrumental stems and lets you compare its processing with the dry music. Nothing here is merged into the shipping game; your ears are the acceptance gate.

Suggested route: try Pyramid Peak first, then Space Mountain, then Candy Cane Canyon, then Vertigo Vents, then Sky Jellyfish, then Steampunk, then Crystal's stronger air shimmer, then Garbage's new musical clangs. After that, revisit the experiments you've already liked. For each, note anything too weak, too loud, or musically distracting.
