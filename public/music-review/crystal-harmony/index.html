What the prototype actually does

The player reads the existing SHA-checked original OGGs, then routes guitar, piano and other through a quiet three-pitch resonator bank. It crossfades between two banks when the strongest pitches change, with a fixed Q ceiling and wet-bus compressor. Turn shimmer off for instant A/B comparison without restarting the song. The simulated landing generates a short, impact-scaled three-note bell based on the currently estimated harmony.

This independent source-time pitch timeline uses 250ms bins from three correlated separated instrument stems. Their tuning estimate was unstable, so the prototype safely uses standard A440 rather than guessing an offset. If pitch evidence is too weak, the resonators and bells go quiet. A pleasing result does not prove chord labels or musical downbeats.
