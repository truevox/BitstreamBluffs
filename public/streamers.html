🎥 Streamers welcome.

Play it. Stream it. Clip it. Monetize your gameplay. Keep the music on.

You have the game's developer's permission to show Bitstream Bluffs gameplay publicly, including its in-game music and sound effects, in your live streams, VODs, reviews, Let's Plays, highlight videos, guides and clips. No advance request or application required.

This permission includes ordinary creator monetization of that gameplay content: advertising revenue, subscriptions, channel memberships, viewer donations, and sponsored streams. We do not require you to turn the soundtrack off or use a special silent “streamer mode.”

What's covered

Normal gameplay capture, editing and commentary with the game's audio as part of the footage. You can record a speedrun, post a ridiculous crash compilation, or make an entire series.

What's not a standalone music license

This permission does not independently license extracted soundtrack files for distribution, resale, remixes, sampling, Content ID registration, or standalone music uploads. Those uses have their own rights questions.

If a platform flags the music

Automated copyright claims can happen despite game-developer permission. If your gameplay stream or VOD is claimed, please report it via our dedicated gameplay-music claim form, with the video URL, song (if known), platform and claimant. Avoid posting personal information or private account data in a public issue.

We'll investigate and help supply a statement of game-developer permission or pursue whitelisting where the underlying music licenses and platform tools allow. We can't guarantee a platform or third-party claimant will immediately honor this policy.

This page describes the developer's permission for gameplay footage. It is not a blanket Creative Commons grant over individual songs or third-party assets, nor a promise of ownership over works beyond the developer's rights.
