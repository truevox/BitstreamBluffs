# Crystal Cavern — source-time harmony sidecar and sympathetic shimmer audition

**2026-11-21 EST / Music Friday · [DRAFT PR #53](https://github.com/truevox/BitstreamBluffs/pull/53) · Original-source research, NOT a released song-score/chord/beat grid or game integration.**

## Why this is better than first obtaining an approved BPM grid

Marvin asked whether CC Friday's proposed Crystal Cavern sympathetic resonance, reduced airborne percussion, and harmony-aware landing bells could use original instrument stems without MIDI re-performance. CC recommended offline pitch-class analysis saved per beat/bar. The original recording *One More Run* has excellent **~163.8 BPM rhythmic periodicity** across original master WAV, MP3, and drums, but **actual source beat-one, meter/bridge, and phase stability remain unverified** in PR #53. The harmonic effect should therefore **not** depend on any presumed beat map.

This version uses an immutable **250ms original-source-time grid** based on exactly **12,000 48kHz master sample frames per bin**. Every frame position is continuous from 0 through exactly 8,380,800 source frames (174.6s). No BPM, quarter notes, 4/4 or beat boundaries are used for extraction, retuning, or bell choice. It is compatible with a gently drifting source *because it follows absolute song playback time*, not a guessed rhythm grid. It must be rechecked for any future source audio or stem-pack revision.

- Analyzed the original **guitar, piano, other** OGG roles from all **15** original 12-second / short-tail game segments, **45 OGG SHA-256 checks** verified byte-for-byte against earlier original-source drum/bass and harmonic research reports. Every decoded segment matches its original source-timeline sample count with **zero unknown/missing terminal analysis samples**. The script reads source OGGs in place; no source files or master WAV audio is copied or changed.
- Decoded transient mono 11,025Hz PCM for analysis only, stitched across the original 15 segment start-frame positions, then performed **whole-song STFT** pitch analysis. Processing on the **continuous timeline**, instead of running independent 12s chromagrams, avoids artificial harmonic-phase/normalization resets at source segment seams.
- Synthetic-triad negative controls revealed that naive spectral whitening inflated background separation noise and yielded misleading near-uniform pitches. Revised analyzer keeps only local frequency peaks with enough immediate frame-level strength, then sums their fractional-strength contributions into **all 12 equal-tempered pitch classes**. This still has harmonic overtone, timbre, instrument-bleed and note-identification ambiguities, and **must not** be treated as actual chord transcription.
- **699** source-time bins cover the original song. **630/699** hold unverified usable pitch-class distributions (minimum exploratory confidence .1); **64** have insufficient original source harmonic evidence and **5** remain weak. More conservative **521/699** pass the audible DSP gate (confidence >=.18 and at least three separately meaningful pitch-class weights). All others suppress retuning/landing-pitch claims.
- The approximate global tuning estimator **failed its stability check** (median ~−1.19 cents but cross-peak MAD ~26.77 cents). The prototype **refuses to infer a real tuning offset** and instead uses standard A440 with an explicit unverified status. If the source is actually detuned in a section, that segment may sound poor; real musical review is required.
- Original 12-second source seam test: for the 13 boundaries with valid chroma evidence on both sides, median adjacent-bin total-variation distance **0.0635** compared with **0.0848** at other successive valid bins. This is a diagnostic comparison, **not** proof of perfect acoustics at every seam, verified note times, or real harmonic continuity. Harmonic changes at genuine section boundaries remain possible.

## New audible source-only A/B prototype

**Preview:** `/music-review/crystal-harmony/` on [Netlify draft PR #53 preview](https://deploy-preview-53--bitstream-bluffs.netlify.app/music-review/crystal-harmony/).

- Choose any of the **12 source-master-suggested** passages. Defaults to 16.4s, where Marvin corrected the source downbeat-lab dropdown/source playback mismatch. The player seamlessly fetches source OGGs across original 12s boundaries, validates **every fetched original instrument OGG fingerprint** against the source-bound review packet and decodes into browser memory. It uses the original six stem roles together, or guitar/piano/other alone, rather than playing synthesized MIDI replacement instruments.
- **Shimmer ON/OFF is genuine realtime A/B without restarting, moving or tempo-stretching the song.** The original dry source remains continuously audible. Only the three harmonic stems feed the wet resonators; vocals and drums are never synthesized/replaced.
- A restrained resonance bank comprises **3 bandpass resonators per bank, two alternating banks, Q = 13 with hard ceiling 16**, switching only when source-time top pitches/strength change meaningfully. New filter frequencies are assigned **while muted**, then the banks **crossfade for 70ms**. Original-signal wet amplitude is capped with a 55%-scale wet controller and a shared dynamics compressor, with **no positive feedback loops**. This is *sympathetic colored shimmer*, not chord-accurate auto-composition or a new music track.
- **Simulate landing bells** triggers three brief decaying sine partials selected from the current unverified source-time pitch classes, amplitude scaled by the manually adjustable impact slider. The notes share the wet-bus compressor. With weak pitch evidence, the processor **does not invent a chord** and simply declines to synthesize bells. This is an audition-only manually triggered effect, **not yet connected to the existing authoritative `EVT.LANDING` game event**.
- A real source-position readout shows estimated pitch classes **as tentative hints**. There are no beat, bar, downbeat or false section labels; no user observations are written to persistent saves or source audio. The standalone sound-design preview is deliberately **not the authoritative in-game validation lab**, as established in `docs/AUDIO-LABS-RESPONSIBILITIES.md`.

## Follow-on measured stereo quality gate — 2026-11-21, source-exact browser DSP

**The audible and offline QA now use the IDENTICAL shared** `public/music-review/crystal-harmony/graph.mjs` **Web Audio DSP implementation**. The visible lab imports it for original-stem resonance and manual landing bells; `tools/music/smoke_crystal_audio_quality.mjs` imports the same graph and renders the exact original SHA-verified OGGs through Chromium `OfflineAudioContext`. No second independent DSP implementation is used for tests. The original master WAV, OGGs and production/game engine remain unmodified. The test depends on the same original sidecar/source hash contract and verifies all **90 source OGG SHA-256 fingerprints** across 15×6 original game-stem clips before rendering.

**Coverage:** all **12 prioritized original-source listening passages** were rendered as **48 stereo 48kHz offline Web Audio outputs**, four variants for each passage: unchanged dry-source baseline; normal wet resonance 0.20; maximum allowed wet 0.55 and a requested full-intensity landing bell; and live-equivalent **45ms-smoothed shimmer A/B toggle** (on at source-relative 2s, off at 5s). The authoring code retains the original source absolute times and 1.5s tail for each isolated excerpt, including all segment seams and the short ending. The sample-level outputs were analyzed *in memory only*; no audio files, stereo masters, song source metadata or releases were produced.

- **0 clipped samples** across the 48 measured outputs. **Largest observed original+max-wet+impact sample peak: 0.309673**, versus full scale 1.0. This is a **sample-peak check, NOT a true-peak/inter-sample certification or loudness mastering pass**.
- Largest normal shimmer whole-excerpt RMS increase versus dry was only **+0.214 dB**, a basic non-perceptually-weighted RMS level metric, **not LUFS**. The wet-vs-dry difference is measurable in all 12 passages, but relatively small and can be intentionally absent/quiet during low-evidence passages.
- Maximum adjacent-sample change in the **wet-only residual** with maximum shimmer+requested bell was **0.017933 full-scale**; when toggling shimmer live with 45ms smoothing, maximum residual adjacent change was **0.004442 full-scale**. Under our conservative exploratory threshold of 0.06, no large newly added sample discontinuity was found. The underlying dry original has its own larger attacks. **A sample delta threshold does not prove that there are no audible clicks or tonal artifacts**; human listening remains necessary.
- Stereo folding to mono was checked: **smallest normal-wet mono RMS / stereo RMS ratio was ~0.903** across these excerpts (a descriptive comparison, not a professional phase-correlation or stereo sound-quality certification).
- When landing was manually requested at 47% of each passage, **8/12** produced three harmony-estimated bell voices; **4/12 intentionally produced zero** where the pitch evidence did not pass its current threshold. This is correct conservative suppression, not a claim that those passages have no real harmony.
- Offline render wall times (~90.5ms median for a ~9s normal wet source passage **on CT223 with Chromium**) are not device CPU load, live audio callback headroom, mobile battery use, or an endorsement for low-powered devices.

**Reproducibility note:** Browser wall-clock elapsed time is emitted only to the diagnostic console because it varies with system load. Tiny (~1e-6) floating-point render differences were also observed on repeat; the fingerprinted metrics JSON explicitly rounds numeric measurements to **1e-4 full-scale**, and its SHA-256 was confirmed identical across two fresh full 48-render passes. This does **not** claim bit-for-bit identical PCM.
