## Implemented architecture

- `ScrapPitchCadence` is a pure reusable event-policy helper shared verbatim by the game and the listening player. Each new Garbage biome entry seeds a fixed PRNG for deterministic, pseudo-random time offsets; while genuinely grounded and moving at contact proxy ≥0.16 (actual sled speed ≥276 px/s), potential clang opportunities arrive at roughly **0.75–2.30 seconds** apart. A request is skipped without backfill if no confident original current bass pitch is available. No `Math.random`, added gameplay timer, beat-grid invention, physics, score or music clock modification. Gameplay reuses its **existing 75ms source music `_pump()`** and checks only when a clang is due. Airborne, pause, menu, source stop, and biome exit suppress the schedule. Reset on actual confirmed Garbage biome transitions, not on repeated same-biome assignments.
- The intact original bass stem receives an early, unity-gain **`scrapPitchProbe`** analyser tap *BEFORE its previously existing bass lowpass/saturation FX*. The tap is almost silent (1e-7 monitor to prevent WebAudio graph optimization); it **does not apply pitch correction** to the original recording. It reuses the tested 4096-FFT `confidentCrystalNotes` estimator: a conservative candidate must be a real current frequency peak with both broadband and local prominence; silence and broadband noise return NONE. Candidates may be overtones rather than correct melody fundamentals; never claim verified song key/chord. No additional always-on second pitch detector loop in the real game.
- The existing `scrapheap` processor on **original drum stem** gains separately adjustable `pitchedClang` and `clangLevel` parameters. A 620ms deterministic pitched-metal buffer starts with the actual measured source note frequency (validated against the pitch helper, not freely accepted hertz), with a jagged 2.67x and 4.15x inharmonic overtone cluster and short noisy shard tail; three-voice overlap cap, explicit cleanup on dispose and pitch-rate change from verified source only. It is **not** a replacement for the Scrapheap inharmonic drum contact resonators, original drum source, physical SPLORP (same 160%), nor the takeoff schlorp (same A / 80%). It only plays while the processor has actual grounded contact (>0.12) and event strength is bounded.
- The audition uses the **same pure `ScrapPitchCadence` and live original bass analyser** and same original five 20s MP3 stems, not duplicated originals or replacement music. Added full dry compare, separate clang intensity 0–200%, occurrence rate 50–200%, automatic on/off, confidence readout and manual Test on next verified-pitch clang. The audition-only manual Test waits up to 4 seconds for a measured note if the source is currently silent. This intentional user-interaction wait is **not** added to actual gameplay. `Play from bass phrase (3s)` avoids confusing sample silence at the start. Separate from the already-liked Scrapheap audition page.

## Non-listening QA and same-biome cadence fix (2026-11-23)

A broad integration review identified a subtle scheduling failure: `StemMusicDirector.setBiome()` reset the seeded clang due time even when its biome ID remained the same, despite the stated intended semantics that a same-biome reapplication must not reset physical state. A repeated biome event could therefore postpone clangs indefinitely. Corrected `setBiome` to preserve the due time on same-biome reapplication, relying on the already-existing confirmed-boundary reset and explicit pause/menu/takeoff resets. Added a direct regression that a same-Garbage ID preserves next scheduled tick/seed while a genuine exit/re-entry correctly resets timing. No effect on already-liked SPLORP, suction, real stem masters or musical pitch selection. Tests pass.
