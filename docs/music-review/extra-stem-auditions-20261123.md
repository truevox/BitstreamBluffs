# Experimental Extra slider for each stem-processing audition

Branch `friday/music-extra-sliders-20261123`; **preview only, not gameplay DSP or a merge to main**.

All thirteen stem-FX audition pages have an independently adjustable `Extra biome bloom` slider from 0–200%, starting at **0%**. Its common helper (`tools/music/AuditionExtra.js`) taps only each audition's processed audio, band-limits it and adds a short delayed, lightly dispersed parallel voice to the existing master. Because the parallel wet gain is strictly **zero at 0%**, the previous sound is preserved at the start, including existing sound effects and dry/processed A/B behavior. This is an additional **source-fed halo of the respective biome output**, not a direct boost to each biome's individual DSP intensity parameter. Takeoff and landing parameter defaults remain as they were. Nonzero Extra can color event audio included on the processed bus; feedback for specific sounds remains a user listening gate.

Auditions: Crystal Cavern, Sky Jellyfish, Space, Steampunk, Pyramid, Candy Cane, Vertigo, Underwater, Micro, Snowpack, Scrapheap, Scrap Clangs and Arcade. The integrated tour lives at `public/listen-today.html` and, in the private CT223 preview, `http://100.115.120.66:8212/listen-today.html`.
