# Underwater Mountain Hydrostatic — draft physics-to-original-stem experiment

Status: **experimental, draft, listening required**. Implementation owner Gal Friday. Issue [#250](https://github.com/truevox/BitstreamBluffs/issues/250), stacked after Scrapheap PR #221 / Snowpack PR #194, following design PR #200 and the owner's Underwater-depth refinements. Original recordings and MIDI-derived biome vocal melodies must never be removed or rewritten.

## Physical truth

- Each **confirmed Underwater biome entry** initializes a terrain-entry elevation reference on the first real finite terrain sample. Terrain/world Y grows positive downward.
- `deepestTerrainDescentPx` is a monotonic high-water mark: `max(previous, terrainY-entryTerrainY, 0)`; **not** the rider's Y, airtime, horizontal speed, or total path distance. Riding uphill cannot undo it. When terrain reaches a new low, stored pressure rises further. `storedPressure01 = 1-exp(-deepest/460px)` is a bounded, soft-knee starting calibration, not a physical hydrostatic-pressure measurement in Pa.
- Jump relief uses **the same real post-physics sled state**: rider Y relative to local terrain, actual signed vertical velocity (negative when ascending), grounded flag. Upward movement temporarily opens filter space; apex retains partial relief; falling restores sound before touchdown. `audiblePressure01 = storedPressure01 * (1-0.90*jumpRelief01)` means the remembered baseline NEVER changes during a jump. Invalid terrain samples hold prior state, and missing vertical motion cannot invent relief.
- **Only confirmed biome exit or a fresh run** resets reference/high-water depth; repeated same-biome events do not. AudioSynth's additive `setHydrostaticMotion` reads post-simulation terrain and sled data from RunScene with no gameplay or ranking mutation. The shared song clock and original instrument source playback are unaffected.

## DSP and listening experiment

- New native Web Audio processor `hydrostatic` runs **on each original instrumental stem**, with bass retaining more dry warmth. Parallel dry/wet paths are bounded. Lowpass cutoff and warm midrange are controlled by **audible pressure** through smooth audio-param automation; dry source retains enough highs for song recognition. Existing separate vocal MIDI lead remains in its own routing, as designed.
- A physically triggered drum-only **0.36-second unpitched pressure-wave/quiet bubbles** one-shot plays on a valid real landing, capped at three concurrent voices. No fabricated beat grid, no random live scheduling, deterministic buffer seed, cleanup on dispose.
- Demo `tools/music/hydrostatic-audition.html` plays **the same original five separated instrument preview stems**, and lets a listener adjust signed terrain descent relative to entry, move uphill without clearing the high-water mark, simulate rising/apex/falling/landing, compare dry, or explicitly exit/reset the biome. Its simulated inputs are labeled and drive the **same pure state policy** as the actual game. The demo is not a substitute for running the game.

## Owner listening correction — stronger pressure contrast, audible landing wave

**Direct user feedback (not an assistant inference):** “More pressure contrast, and I didn't hear the pressure wave.” This replaces any earlier implication that sample-headroom proof is sufficient. It concerns **Underwater Mountain only**, not Marvin's liked Scrapheap takeoff A/80% and landing SPLORP 160% (both unchanged).

**Diagnosis / design changes (Gal Friday recommendations, not owner-selected final settings):** the old deep Hydrostatic mix still carried 26% of an unfiltered dry stem path at maximum pressure, while the unpitched landing wave was only 360 ms with amplitude coefficient `(.08+.22*strength)`. The new deep mix makes the retained dry intelligibility anchor roughly 6.5% of the original stem at maximum pressure with the revised **190% depth-contrast default**; a stronger 2-stage low-pass with warm 320-Hz midrange and smoother 65-ms automation makes the ascent/descend spectral contrast more noticeable. This new `contrast` control is bounded **60–220%** independently of physics depth memory; it never changes or resets the stored terrain descent. Independently, default jumping relief is raised to 90% of computed relief energy (limited by true height/velocity phase), still without modifying stored pressure.

The source-independent wave now lasts 580 ms instead of 360 ms: an audible lower-mid **water-hammer thump (roughly 145 Hz) + resonant body (roughly 300 Hz) + noisy pressure rush + five bubbling air releases**, all deterministic from a fixed seed. Independent `waveStrength` adjusts **0–240%** with **150% provisional default**, and a **Test pressure wave** button allows repetition without creating a fake landing or restarting the original song. In the actual game, only the real noncrash landing event can trigger this wave; at most three overlapping waves are allowed, with explicit disconnect/stop on DSP graph disposal. The existing original instrument stems, stereo masters, vocal MIDI, timing, terrain memory and game physics are unchanged. These are sound effects, not a recording of actual fluid or a musical replacement stem.

**Chromium five-original-stem, same three-second sample evidence:** old vs new deep-pressure dry-difference RMS **0.013122 → 0.016574**; rising relief vs fully pressured difference **0.012384 → 0.016163**; landing-wave difference against same-depth mix **0.006152 → 0.050319** (roughly 8.18×). The new wave-off control produces difference RMS **1.27e-9**, confirming that the slider reaches zero. At new defaults, dry sampled peak **0.17309**, deep-pressure **0.17309**, landing **0.37185**; max-wave one-shot sampled peak **0.58329** and **three closely spaced maximum waves peak 0.70224**; no nonfinite samples, no clipping in this measured sample. The two independent sliders are enabled on the browser audition. These are sampled, short-passage checks only; do not claim full-song true peak, perceived loudness, device coverage or owner approval.

**Acceptance:** human audition of maximum-depth pressure vs dry, jump relief, falling reimposition, and **Test pressure wave** at default and stronger settings. Retain draft status until owner re-listening.
