# Crystal Cavern — Harmonic Lattice original-stem sympathetic resonance

**Status:** isolated experimental draft; *not merged*, not yet approved by Marvin's ears. Issue [#256](https://github.com/truevox/BitstreamBluffs/issues/256); implementation stacked above Micro Mountain draft #255 (then Hydrostatic #252, Scrapheap #221, Snowpack #194), music design PR #200. Authorship: this implementation, its provisional settings and interpretations are **Gal Friday's**, not a fabricated owner decision.

## Musical and physics contracts

- Crystal Cavern must sound **consonant, sustained and harmonic**; distinct from Garbage's inharmonic junk metal and Micro's tight plastic enclosure. It uses bounded **native high-Q chromatic bandpass resonance**, 24 equally tempered bins on *each original tonal stem* (bass 130–494 Hz/C3–B4; guitar/piano/other 262–988 Hz/C4–B5), each mode receiving the live source signal. A short damped echo/feedback pathway gives the physical lattice a tail. Equal-temperament bins are **candidate physical resonant frequencies, NOT an inferred song key**; their audible excitation derives only from the actual source frequencies in the recording. No new notes are scheduled by the bank, and the original dry song always stays present.
- **Important actual sample limitation discovered, not glossed over:** for this specific 20-second original-5-stem Pope preview, measured FFmpeg 12-second RMS is about **drums −20.9 dBFS, bass −30.0 dBFS, piano −84.5 dBFS, guitar −89.8 dBFS, other −92.4 dBFS**. The initial implementation relying only on piano/guitar/other produced almost ZERO audible new resonance, which was caught by the real-five-stem Chromium OfflineAudioContext regression. The corrected version includes the clearly audible **original bass**, preserving its original dry waveform, and **only the bass wet feed** gets gentle harmonics-enhancing saturation to excite physical modes. That is derived processing, not a source replacement or artificial instrument performance. More melodic original-stem sources still need separate future audition and verification.
- In grounded Crystal, original drums retain their full source path; in true physical flight, `StemMusicDirector.setAirborne` applies `openness=1` to all four tonal-original crystallattice processors and independently lowers original drum `crystalPercussion` FX gain to 48%. When landed, openness and drum gain return. These are **authoritative real sled-state transitions**, not faked BPM/onset detection. The game's existing other airborne drum mixing remains in place; Crystal is an additional separately scoped coloration. There is no `setInterval`, timer or background per-frame pitch scan.
- On a **valid actual noncrash touchdown**, `impactFromLanding` causes a ~1-second swell of the **already source-fed** modal signal; otherwise it cannot generate notes by itself. A short optional high-register crystal cascade can sound ONLY if a live `AnalyserNode` on the ORIGINAL bass stream measures a sufficiently prominent frequency. Detection uses 4096 FFT bins, broadband median as noise floor, 12dB global prominence, 4.5dB local peak prominence, peaks above −68 dBFS, and produces at most two widely separated pitches in C4–B5. It may select a bass overtone rather than a melody fundamental; report them as **measured spectral candidates**, never certified key/chord notes. Weak audio, broadband noise or silence must return *no synthetic notes*. No random note seeding, guessed chord, fake beat grid, or MIDI replacement.
- Pitched physical cascade voices are at most four simultaneous quiet sine chimes, 1.4 sec each, frequency from supported measured note with stagger 55ms. They are stopped/disconnected on teardown. The resonant wet effect uses its **own** thresholded DynamicsCompressor (no alteration to the dry original) because the fully open 200% test initially clipped above full scale. The final wet-only compressor lowers the peak while leaving the original dry stem unprocessed. Source waveforms, original stereo master recordings, separate preexisting MIDI vocal leads, scoring, gameplay physics, game music clock and the other four owner-liked DSP defaults are unchanged.

## Source-backed QA evidence (Nov 23, 2026)

- Deterministic pure chroma tests reject missing/NaN/broadband/silent spectra and identify only explicitly injected measured strong notes. Native DSP tests cover modal setup, never-synthesize-on-no-confidence, actual frequency selection, bounded four-voice cap, per-effect cleanup. Director integration tests cover source presets, grounded→airborne once, pending landing impulse, per-biome reset. Vite production build and Chromium player smoke also gate changes.
- **Actual live playback** (real bass MP3 in running Chromium `AudioContext`, not a synthetic fixture): detector returned no candidates in bass silence at ~0.3/1.5/2.7 sec, then confidence-supported candidate MIDI 68/61 near 3sec, MIDI 65 near 3.3–3.9sec, MIDI 72 around 6.6sec; these are frequency peaks/overtones, not a verified song-key analysis. This confirms the live stream can supply candidates and no-notes fallback in distinct sections.
- **Five original instrumental MP3 stems, Chrome OfflineAudioContext at 48kHz, 5-second sample:** post-wet-compression dry mix RMS **0.02614**, grounded Crystal **0.05492**, airborne **0.07076**, source-fed impact at a **REAL bass phrase 3.2sec** RMS **0.05733**. Compared on the matched active interval, lattice-vs-zero-strength difference RMS **0.00558**, airborne-vs-ground **0.01396**, physical landing-vs-ground **0.01257**. The previous false test at 1.5sec (bass near silence) showed almost no impact, corrected to a note-bearing phrase; this is not a manufactured success, rather it exposes source-dependent behavior. Dry sampled peak **0.17309**, grounded **0.25909**, airborne **0.31456**, impact **0.28370**. Maximum 200% air bloom sampled peak **0.38190** after the wet-only compressor (previously **1.03168**, clipped). No nonfinite samples. These are limited excerpts/sample peaks, not whole-song integrated loudness, true peak or mobile/headphone acceptance.
- No added synced beat/detected key metadata; no explicit owner acceptance yet. The audition must preserve original stems, allow grounded/airborne/dry and a direct landing cascade, and reveal current bass pitch evidence honestly when no confidence is present.

## Listening gate

Marvin to listen at default resonance **100%**, compare dry against a grounded cave, take off to hear percussion retreat and resonance bloom, land around an audible bass phrase to hear the source-led cascade, then try the slider from 0–200%. If the cave sounds merely louder or muddy, or the tiny piano-derived synth chimes overpower the real recording, adjust before any merge. The baseline accompaniment for this preview is bass/drums-heavy, not a representative full-tonal musical arrangement, and a second more melodic source is necessary for complete Crystal acceptance.

## Owner listening update — 150% resonance, airborne bloom too subtle (2026-11-23)

**Direct owner feedback:** “That is crazy! Can we use that tech to make random on pictch clangs in Garbage too? I turned the resonance up to 150. Sounded good! I didn't hear much air bloom though.” The value **150%** is the owner's actual liked Crystal resonance slider setting. “Didn't hear much air bloom” is a negative auditory observation; previous airborne evidence included drum ducking and did not independently prove bloom audibility. Random on-pitch Garbage clangs are a distinct new requested feature, to be tracked separately, rather than mixing the sound identities of Crystal and Scrapheap.

**Gal Friday correction:** original-source Crystal resonance default now `strength:1.5` across all four tonal stems and the preview. The grounded tonal processing is otherwise unchanged. True airborne state now additionally (1) strengthens the original-source-wet harmonic envelope, (2) extends the feedback decay (bounded feedback 0.60 grounded, 0.78 airborne), and (3) adds a filtered **~950Hz+** high-harmonic wet shimmer derived only from the existing modal signals, with its own adjustable **Airborne shimmer boost** (0–250%, **160% provisional starting value**). The original dry signal still travels at unity gain; no added beat, random notes, original master edit or separate MIDI lead edit. The wet-only compressor remains in place for headroom. The airborne shimmer control does NOT change grounded resonance or the source-pitch confidence gate.

**Five actual original stems, Chromium OfflineAudioContext, 5s 48kHz correction verification:** at 150% resonance, dry RMS ~0.02614, grounded Crystal RMS **0.06113**, full airborne **0.09108**, and max 200% resonance + max 250% shimmer airborne sample peak **0.44822** (no sampled clipping, no nonfinite values). Crucially, **with drum level held constant** (no drum duck), actual flight-vs-ground difference RMS **0.03367**, and the *additional shimmer path by itself* contributes difference RMS **0.01315** against identical airborne settings with only shimmer turned off. This now proves a distinct signal independent of the existing drum duck; it is **not yet proof of human perceptual acceptance** or a full-song true-peak / mobile-speaker certification. All 172 focused music/audio/chroma tests remain green.
