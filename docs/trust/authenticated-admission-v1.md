# #135 — server-only authenticated admission reference

This is a private development slice, not a public service. The trusted coordinator supplies an authenticated account and server-selected course. It must never derive either from unchecked client assertions. Separate Ed25519 public-key allowlists authenticate simulation, entitlement and consent envelopes. Every signed body binds role, account, challenge, exact canonical claim digest and validity window. All three payloads must also pass the existing dual-proof policy. A server-generated challenge pins course, loadout, ruleset and verifier executable. SQLite serializes admission and records one candidate verdict and receipt audit digests. No wallet, reward or publication is mutated.

Development choices, not approved production policy: Node 22 SQLite (experimental), 15-minute challenge TTL, globally unique run and tape digests. Identical legitimate input tapes could collide; production deduplication and tournament deadlines need separate review. Free queue delay must not disadvantage players. Keys, storage, migrations, clock rollback, revocation, rate limits and operational controls require production design. Audit hashes are not an evidence archive. No endpoint or actual issuer is implemented. No secrets or real accounts are used in tests.

The local/provisional paths are unchanged; this adapter admits only final verified-public claims. The next layers are independent real-engine replay, authoritative wallet and gear receipts, authenticated sessions, explicit consent and provisional publication, blind peer coordination and paid-route parity, then ranking and reward settlement. Peer results are not intrinsically trusted. No public rollout until separately reviewed and authorized. Keep #135 open.
