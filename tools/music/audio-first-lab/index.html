What's real and what's still a draft

For Pope and One More Run, the lead instruments are our existing complete Gentle-score performances. Every Hacker (Part 2) keeps its original Maxi vocal unchanged; its new eleven guitar instruments are sampled musical performances, but automatic guitar note extraction may need ear correction. The bass V2 and Every Hacker featured guitar are note-guided real sampled SoundFont instruments, though their automatically detected notes require listening verification. The airborne drums and the featured tracks in Pope and One More Run are still source-preserving timbre prototypes. All versions share the original song clock. We audition their musical identities before investing in that expensive step.
