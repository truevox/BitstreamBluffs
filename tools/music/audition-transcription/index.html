What you're hearing

The Pure SheetSage −12 version you liked already uses the same V3 FluidR3 church-organ program (GM 19), attack layer, saturation and underlying texture as the original V3. It fixes note pitches and onsets to SheetSage’s 68-note score. The NEW hybrids keep those notes and exact timestamps, then borrow only measured micro-pitch movement from inside each original V3 note—gently (up to 32 cents) or more expressively (up to 70 cents). V3’s note-center errors and octave jumps are deliberately excluded.

This is a pitch-and-note-timing experiment, not a sound-design competition. The SheetSage2 weights are noncommercial research assets; the output is for private evaluation only. The Basic Pitch monophonic cleanup and octave shift are hypotheses, not corrections validated by a musician. No new music was integrated into the game.

Engineering source: Bitstream Bluffs adaptive music draft PR.
